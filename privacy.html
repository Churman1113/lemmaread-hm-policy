一、导语

欢迎使用 lemma词元（以下简称"本应用"）。本应用是一款多语言词典工具，提供法语、西班牙语、德语等语言的词典查询、词汇学习及社区交流功能。我们非常重视您的个人信息和隐私保护，本隐私政策将向您说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。请在使用本应用前仔细阅读本政策。

二、我们如何收集和使用您的个人信息

1. 账号信息（邮箱地址）

当您注册或登录本应用时，我们会收集您的邮箱地址，用于账号注册、登录及身份验证。密码经加密处理后存储于服务器。

2. 社区内容（帖子、评论、词单）

当您在社区功能中发布帖子、评论或分享词单时，我们会收集并展示您发布的内容，用于社区功能的发布、展示与互动。您发布的公开内容对其他用户可见，您可以随时删除。

3. 本地数据

词典查询记录、阅读进度、单词本、书签和统计等数据默认仅存储在您的设备本地，我们不会主动收集或上传这些本地数据。

三、设备权限

本应用仅申请"网络访问"权限，用于账号登录及社区内容的获取与发布。本应用不申请位置、通讯录、相册、麦克风、摄像头等任何敏感权限。

四、对未成年人的保护

本应用面向一般用户。如您为未成年人，请在监护人指导下使用本应用。若我们在未获得监护人同意的情况下收集了未成年人的个人信息，将尽快删除相关数据。

五、与第三方共享

本应用使用 Supabase 云服务提供商托管账号信息和社区内容数据。除为实现上述功能所必需外，我们不会向任何第三方出售或共享您的个人信息。

六、第三方 SDK

本应用未集成任何第三方统计、广告、推送类 SDK。

七、管理您的个人信息

您可以在应用内"设置"页面退出登录；社区功能中，您可以删除自己发布的帖子、评论和词单。您也可以通过文末联系方式联系我们，行使查询、更正、删除个人信息的权利。

八、信息存储地点及期限

您的账号信息和社区内容存储于 Supabase 云端服务器；词典数据、阅读记录存储于您的设备本地。账号数据在您注销后删除，社区内容在您删除后移除。

九、如何联系我们

如您对本隐私政策有任何疑问，可通过以下方式联系我们：churman1113@outlook.com

1. Introduction

Welcome to lemma词元 (the "App"), a multi-language dictionary tool providing dictionary lookup, vocabulary learning and community features for French, Spanish, German and more. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information.

2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Account Information (Email Address)

When you register or log in, we collect your email address for account registration, login and identity verification. Passwords are stored in encrypted form.

Community Content (Posts, Comments, Wordlists)

When you publish posts, comments or share wordlists in the community, we collect and display such content for community features. Public content is visible to other users and can be deleted by you at any time.

Local Data

Dictionary lookups, reading progress, vocabulary, bookmarks and statistics are stored locally on your device by default and are not collected or uploaded.

3. Device Permissions

The App only requests the "Internet" permission for account login and community features. It does not request sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

4. Protection of Minors

The App is intended for general users. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a guardian. If we become aware that we have collected personal information of minors without guardian consent, we will delete such data as soon as possible.

5. Sharing with Third Parties

We use Supabase cloud services to host account information and community content. Except as necessary to provide the features above, we do not sell or share your personal information with any third party.

6. Third-Party SDKs

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or push SDKs.

7. Managing Your Personal Information

You can log out in the Settings page, and delete posts, comments and wordlists you published. You may also contact us to exercise your rights to access, correct or delete personal information.

8. Storage Location and Retention

Account information and community content are stored on Supabase cloud servers; dictionary data and reading records are stored locally on your device. Account data is deleted after account cancellation, and community content is removed upon deletion.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: churman1113@outlook.com

10. Effective Date

This policy is effective as of August 8, 2026.
